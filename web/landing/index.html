Business communications
on your infrastructure.

Zeus delivers business communications on your own infrastructure: phone numbers ordered and provisioned through VoIP.ms in seconds, a web + PWA softphone over WebRTC, SMS and unified messaging, AvantFax digital faxing, AI voicemail summaries, Stripe billing and reseller white-label — fronted by a Next.js portal with Authentik SSO and Nginx Proxy Manager HTTPS.
